CP/M-86 material in this repository
===================================

dr/CPM.H86, dr/BIOS.A86, dr/CBIOS.A86, dr/SINGLES.LIB and dr/DEBLOCK.LIB are
Digital Research's CP/M-86 1.1 system files (c8611src.zip from the "Unofficial
CP/M Web Site", http://www.cpm.z80.de/).

cpmfiles/*.CMD and cpmfiles/HELP.HLP are Digital Research's CP/M-86 1.1
utilities, from the same site (c8611src.zip and the IBM PC CP/M-86 1.1 set,
cpm86pc.zip).

Also from that site, all Digital Research products:
  cpmfiles/RASM86, LINK86, LIB86, XREF86 (Programmer's Utilities 1.1) and
  cpmfiles/B/* (DR C 1.11): drc_86.zip
  cpmfiles/BASIC.CMD, BASIC.ME (Personal BASIC 1.1): pbasic86.zip
  cpmfiles/C/* (PL/I-86 1.0): pli86.zip
  cpmfiles/D/CB86.* and the CBASIC samples (CB86 2.0): cbasic86.zip
  cpmfiles/D/ASM.COM, LOAD.COM, DUMP.COM: CP/M 2.2 (see pocket8086-cpm)
The HELLO.C, HELLO.BAS and HELLO80.ASM samples are original (MIT).
